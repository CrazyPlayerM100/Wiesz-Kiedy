## 2. What the app is
Wiesz Kiedy is a free public transport information app. It offers:
- timetables and departures from stops,
- live vehicle positions,
- trip planning and guidance during a trip,
- disruption notices and punctuality statistics.

It does not sell tickets or handle any payments.

The app is independent. It is not run by, or affiliated with, any transit operator, transport authority or city. Operator and line names are used only to say where the information comes from and what it is about.

Some features need permissions (location, notifications), which you can withdraw at any time. A timetable downloaded earlier works offline.
