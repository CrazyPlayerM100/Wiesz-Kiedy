# Wiesz Kiedy privacy policy

Effective from 5 November 2026.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data
The controller is the author of the Wiesz Kiedy app (Mateusz Cieślik). Contact for anything about privacy: support@wieszkiedy.pl.

## 2. In short
- No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and no data is ever sold.
- Your favourite stops, saved places, search history and settings stay on your phone.
- Your location is used on the phone. Only the coordinates a feature you ask for needs (a walking route, the address of a point) reach the server, without any user identifier.
- Crash reports are sent only if you agree.

## 3. What we process, why, and on what basis

### 3.1. Data on your device
Favourites, saved places (such as Home and Work), recent searches, followed lines, reminders and settings are stored on the phone. The author has no access to them. Settings may be included in Android's backup (Google) if you have it switched on; Google handles it under your account's terms. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it removes them.

### 3.2. Location
With your permission, the app uses your location to show you on the map, find nearby stops, plan a trip "from my location" and guide you during a trip (also with the screen off, with a visible notification). Your location is not stored on the server or linked to you. Only the coordinates of the points needed are sent to the app's server, to:
- work out a walking route (start and end of the walk),
- turn a point into an address ("Karmelicka 12").
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you ask for). You can withdraw the permission at any time in the phone's settings; the app keeps working without location.

### 3.3. Place search
What you type in search is sent to the app's server and from there (or, when the server does not answer, straight from the phone) to OpenStreetMap-based search services: Photon (komoot GmbH, Germany) and Nominatim (OpenStreetMap Foundation, United Kingdom). Only the search text and the chosen city's area are sent, nothing about you. Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### 3.4. Timetables, map and live vehicles
To download timetables, vehicle positions and map tiles, the phone connects to the app's server, to the OpenFreeMap map server and, if the app's server fails, directly to transit operators and data providers (transit authorities, mkuran.pl, zbiorkom.live). Like any internet connection, this shows these servers your IP address. Basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

### 3.5. Server logs
The app's server logs the IP address, time and the file requested (without query parameters, so without coordinates or search text). The logs serve security and abuse detection and are deleted after 14 days. Basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest: keeping the service secure).

Address search answers are cached on the server for up to 14 days so as not to burden those services. The cache holds the search texts and coordinates of the requests, but no IP addresses or anything else that would link a request to a person.

### 3.6. Crash reports (only with consent)
If you agree, after a crash the app sends a report: the app and Android versions, the phone model, the chosen city, the time and a technical description of the error (stack trace). It holds no location, device identifiers or anything you typed. The server does not store the IP address with the report. Reports are deleted after 90 days. Basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), which you can withdraw in Settings → Data and offline without affecting earlier processing.

### 3.7. Punctuality statistics
Line and stop delay statistics are computed on the server only from the operators' public vehicle data. They contain no data about users.

## 4. Who receives data
No data is sold or shared for marketing. Recipients (to the extent described above) are:
- the server provider: Oracle Cloud (data centre in the European Union, Frankfurt), acting as a processor,
- Cloudflare, Inc. (USA), whose network carries the connections to the app's server (wieszkiedy.pl), acting as a processor; it sees the IP address and the data passed to the extent needed to deliver it and protect against attacks; transfer to the USA on the basis of the European Commission's EU-US Data Privacy Framework decision, in which Cloudflare takes part,
- the services your phone connects to: OpenFreeMap (map), komoot GmbH (Photon), OpenStreetMap Foundation (Nominatim), transit operators and transit data providers,
- Google (Google Play location services and Android backup, if you use them).
The OpenStreetMap Foundation is in the United Kingdom, which the European Commission has found to ensure an adequate level of data protection.

## 5. Your rights
You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, to object to processing based on legitimate interest and to withdraw consent. As the app links no data to an account or identifier, in most cases we cannot tell which data is yours (Art. 11 GDPR); if you give more details (such as when a crash happened), we will try to help. You may complain to the Polish supervisory authority, Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority where you live.

## 6. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## 7. Security
Connections to the app's server are encrypted (HTTPS). The server keeps only the data described here, for the time given here.

## 8. Changes
Significant changes to this policy will be announced in the app. The current version is always in Settings → Legal and at wieszkiedy.pl/privacy-policy.
